Before you begin.

Will my progress be saved?

Your current exam progress is stored in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data or changing devices does not transfer your attempt. No student account is created.

How does UBT listening work?

Press a question or choice audio button once to play its recording twice. It becomes unavailable after playback for that attempt. Audio does not begin automatically.

Can I download my results?

After completing an exam, download the formatted results page. Open that file and use your browser’s Print option to save a PDF.

Are these official examinations?

No. These are preparation tools. Study resources retain their source credits. Follow current HRDK and DMW announcements for official requirements and assessments.
