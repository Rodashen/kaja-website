A little about Kaja.

Korean language learning with personal guidance, cultural understanding, and a community that supports your next step.

Our mission.

We help learners work toward their dreams and career goals through quality language training and an engaging, enjoyable learning experience.

Our vision.

We aim to be a center of excellence in language learning, helping every learner reach their potential and make a positive difference wherever they go.

What we teach.

  • EPS-TOPIK preparation, including Korean language skills and exam strategies.
  • Structured speaking, listening, reading, and writing courses.
  • Korean cultural workshops covering food, music, art, and traditions.
  • Customized language and cultural training for individuals and organizations.
  • Flexible online learning with interactive lessons.
  • Preparation for Korean proficiency tests, including TOPIK.

Why learn with Kaja?

  • Experienced teachers, smaller classes, and personalized coaching.
  • Online and face-to-face learning options.
  • Lessons in Korean culture and etiquette alongside the language.
  • EPS-TOPIK textbook study, mock exams, and pre-exam review.
  • Guidance with EPS and TOPIK registration.
